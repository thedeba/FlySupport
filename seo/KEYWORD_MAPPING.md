## Search intent and risk notes

- **Primary opportunity:** specific, non-branded travel-information queries that match the site's actual service topics. The commercial pages should explain the referral model rather than pretend to be the airline or ticket seller.
- **Branded queries:** focus on AirNexa brand and disclosure pages. No verified airline partnership exists in the project, so airline customer-service and airline-name keyword targets are excluded.
- **Potential overlap:** `/flight-cancellation` and `/cancellation-refund-policy` have related themes. Keep the former focused on general cancellation next steps and the latter focused on the distinction between airline ticket refunds and third-party provider fees. If they cannot be kept distinct, consolidate after reviewing traffic and backlinks in Search Console.
- **Potential overlap:** `/flight-changes` and `/airline-canceled-flight` both cover rebooking. Keep flight changes about traveler-requested itinerary changes and airline-canceled flight about airline-initiated disruptions.
- Avoid generic “customer service phone number” phrases that could imply official airline support. Avoid exact-match keyword lists, city/airline doorway pages, and unsupported “emergency,” “guaranteed,” “instant,” or “official” claims.

## Research method to complete with account access

Use Google Search Console query data after the site has impressions, then compare related phrase variants in Google Ads Keyword Planner or a licensed SEO tool. Record the country, date range, source, and whether metrics are impressions, average monthly searches, or third-party estimates. Validate intent against current search results before changing page targets. Do not use the Search Console performance report as a forecast of total search demand.
