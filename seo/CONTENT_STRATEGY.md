# AirNexa content strategy

## Audience and scope

The current site is for U.S. travelers looking for general information about flight booking, ticket changes, cancellations, missed flights, and last-minute travel. AirNexa is an independent affiliate and lead-generation website; it does not operate flights, make airline decisions, or control provider availability and fees.

Build a small reference library only when each page can offer original, current, useful guidance. Do not create one near-identical page per airline, city, or disruption phrase.

## Editorial review process

1. Define the reader's question and the page's unique purpose.
2. Research time-sensitive airline rules from current primary sources, such as the U.S. Department of Transportation and the responsible airline or ticket seller.
3. Record source URLs, access date, and the specific factual claims each source supports.
4. Draft in plain language and separate general information from provider-specific services or terms.
5. Check that AirNexa is not described as an airline, ticket seller, travel agency, or official airline representative.
6. Verify links, title/description, headings, canonical, disclosures, and internal links.
7. Publish a “Last reviewed” date only after a real review; schedule another review when the cited rules may change.
